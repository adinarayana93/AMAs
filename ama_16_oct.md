# AMA - 16 Oct

## 1. What are the main components of the Claude API?

The main components include messages, models, and parameters that control how Claude responds.

## 2. What is a session?

A session is a period of interaction where the user and AI exchange messages while working on a task.

## 3. What is delegation?

Delegation means assigning a task or part of a task to another agent or tool.

## 4. What is the difference between generative AI and traditional AI?

Generative AI creates new content, while traditional AI mainly classifies data, makes predictions, or follows specific rules.

## 5. What is the difference between a search engine and generative AI?

A search engine finds existing information from sources. Generative AI creates a response based on the prompt and its learned patterns.

## 6. What is context management?

Context management means selecting and organizing the information an AI needs to answer accurately.

## 7. What are connectors?

Connectors link an AI assistant to external apps or services so it can access their data or use their features.
